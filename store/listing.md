## Full description (2414/4000)

```text
CloudLock is an app blocker that teaches you something. Turn every urge to scroll into a five-second look at the sky.

Pick the apps that eat your time, such as social media, video or games. When you open one, CloudLock first shows a photo of a cloud you have learned, with four names to choose from.

HOW IT WORKS
• Right answer: the app opens for the time you chose (10 minutes by default).
• Wrong answer: a short pause shows the right name and how to tell the two apart, so tapping at random doesn't pay off.
• Changed your mind? "I don't need this app right now" is always one tap away, and a few emergency unlocks a day are there when you really need one.

LEARN A FEW CLOUDS A DAY
• A short daily lesson introduces new clouds, then a quick exam checks them.
• Spaced review brings each cloud back after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days until you have mastered it.
• Wrong answers are real look-alikes, such as shelf cloud vs roll cloud, cumulonimbus vs towering cumulus, altocumulus vs cirrocumulus, so you learn the differences that matter.
• Field clues on every card: height, what it looks like, what it is made of, the weather it brings, and the one feature that gives it away.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 36 clouds from the WMO International Cloud Atlas: the ten main types, their species, and rare skies like mammatus, roll clouds, noctilucent and mother-of-pearl clouds.
• A daily streak, a collection with milestones, and your Sky IQ.
• Light and dark mode.

FREE, WITH AN OPTIONAL UPGRADE
• Free: Lock up to 2 apps and learn the ten main cloud types.
• CloudLock Plus (one-time purchase, no subscription): Unlimited locked apps and all 36 clouds, including species and rare clouds.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No account, no ads, no analytics. Everything stays on your phone and works offline.

PERMISSIONS
Locking apps is the core feature of CloudLock. To do it, it asks for:
• Usage access, to see which app is in front.
• Display over other apps, to show the question over a locked app.
• A notification, so Android keeps the lock running.
This information never leaves your phone.

SAFETY
CloudLock is for learning, not forecasting. In stormy weather, follow your local weather service and take shelter.

Photos come from Flickr photographers under Creative Commons licences; every photo is credited in the app.

Website: https://www.sinadehesh.com/cloudlock/
Privacy policy: https://www.sinadehesh.com/cloudlock/privacy/
```
